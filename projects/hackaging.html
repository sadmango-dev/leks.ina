Hackaging Hackathon - Longevity AI Agent

In October, my team and I participated in the hackaging.ai hackathon, where we created an openAI wrapper agent to assist researchers in longevity.
The agent has access to deterministic functions, can access databases, answer questions about specific aging clocks and articles. I worked on the fronted part of the project, as well as general project management and planning.
It was a great experience, and it really inspired me to continue exploring the intersection of AI and longevity research, as well as honing my skills in ML and AI agent systems.
